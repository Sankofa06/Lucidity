Mira is an early native AI studio that connects to machines and model endpoints you configure.

Before contacting support

  1. Confirm the configured machine and service are running.
  2. Verify the device can reach the endpoint over the selected network.
  3. Record the Mira version/build and visible error.

Protect private information

Do not send API keys, tokens, private prompts, generated media, endpoint snapshots, machine names, or private addresses.
